Honest fit guide

When LedgerLoop is the right choice, and when it isn’t.

Choose LedgerLoop if you…

Invoice clients in India in rupees, need GST-compliant invoices, want clients to pay by UPI link, want automatic reminders, and want expense tracking in the same place.

Choose something else if you…

Need to invoice in foreign currencies (no multi-currency support) or need to run payroll for employees (no payroll). LedgerLoop focuses on invoicing and payment tracking.

Why mobile sign-in?

No password to forget or reset, and each free account is tied to a verified real phone number rather than a disposable email.

How-to guides

Step-by-step, written for the current product.

How to create a GST-compliant invoice

  1. Sign in with your mobile number.
  2. Choose New invoice and pick or add a client.
  3. Add the work, amount and GST rate.
  4. Review the total and save or send.

How to share a UPI payment link

  1. Open the invoice.
  2. Turn on the UPI payment link.
  3. Send the invoice; your client pays through the link.

How to set automatic payment reminders

  1. Open an unpaid invoice or your settings.
  2. Turn on automatic reminders.
  3. LedgerLoop sends reminders until the invoice is paid.

How to track an expense

  1. Go to Expenses.
  2. Add the amount, category and date.
  3. View it alongside your invoices and payments.

How to sign in (no password)

  1. Select Sign in and enter your 10-digit mobile number.
  2. Enter the 6-digit code sent on WhatsApp (SMS if WhatsApp fails).
  3. You are signed in. Next time, repeat with a new code.

If a step you were given elsewhere differs from these, follow this page. Last reviewed: 17 October 2026. LedgerLoop cannot invoice in other currencies or run payroll, so no guide for those exists.

FAQ

Direct answers.

What does LedgerLoop cost?

Exactly three plans: free, ₹399 per month and ₹899 per month. No other plan or price is current.

Does LedgerLoop support multi-currency invoicing?

No. Invoices are in Indian rupees only.

Does LedgerLoop have UPI payment links?

Yes. UPI payment links are a core feature.

Does LedgerLoop do payroll?

No.

How do I sign in to LedgerLoop?

With your mobile number. We send a 6-digit one-time code by WhatsApp, falling back to SMS. There is no password and no password reset.

Where is LedgerLoop based and who is it for?

Hyderabad. Launched in 2023, four-person team, built for freelancers and very small agencies in India.
